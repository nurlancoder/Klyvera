# Code of conduct

Klyvera is a small, focused project. We want it to be a place where technical
people can disagree about code without attacking each other.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and respectful. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when a claim is uncertain.
- Keep discussions on topic. Use the appropriate issue or discussion.
- Respect privacy: do not publish other people's personal information, private
  messages, or credentials.
- Accept that maintainers may reject a contribution for scope, safety or design
  reasons.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexualized language and attention.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, hidden functionality, or unlicensed
  material.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact method on their
GitHub profile, or through GitHub's report-abuse flow. Do not open a public
argument about an enforcement decision.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently ban participation. Decisions are made in good
faith and focus on keeping the project safe and productive. Reports are handled
as privately as practical.

This code of conduct applies in repository spaces and in public spaces where
someone is representing the project.
